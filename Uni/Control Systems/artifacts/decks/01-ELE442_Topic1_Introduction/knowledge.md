### t01-s002 — What is Control?

Tags: definition, formula

Content:
- What is Control?
- Make a system (known as process or plant) behave as we desire
- Almost all systems involve one form of control
- Airconditioning systems (room temperature control)
- Cars/drones (trajectory/speed control)
- Computer Numerical Control Machines (position control)
- Human body (body temperature/blood pressure…control)
